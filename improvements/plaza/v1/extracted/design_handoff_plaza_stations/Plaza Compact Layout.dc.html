A smaller Plaza: every prop within 4 seconds

Today the square is 72 m across and the props stand on a 20 m arc, so most of what a visitor sees is empty paving. The three layouts below shrink the square to a stage, keep the fountain, mosaic ring, festoons, pastel houses and return portal, and drop the hidden rooftop and the seed lever. Distances use the current walk speed of 4.5 m/s.

Blender export rules

Metres, Y up on export, front faces +Z, pivot on the ground at the footprint centre.
One file per model in assets-src/models/, prefixed plaza-: plaza-fountain.glb, plaza-house-a…f.glb, plaza-corner.glb, plaza-arch.glb.
Stucco, shutter and awning faces in separate vertex-colour groups named stucco, shutter, awning, so STUCCO and SHUTTERS keep tinting per house.
An empty named collider box per model sets the footprint; the loader reads it instead of hand-written AABBs.
House fronts sit exactly on the model's +Z face at z = depth / 2, so houseRow places them flush on FACADE.
Then npm run assets:optimize as today.

Removed with this change

Rooftop house, 17-step stair and guard rails in plaza.ts, plus their tests in plaza.spec.ts.
Seed lever on the Plaza. It is placed by the shared project scene, so the Plaza needs a way to opt out.
Open streets to the hills: three of four close (1a, 1b) or all four (1c). The hill backdrop only shows above the roofs.
third-person-clearance.spec.ts should be rerun: with 3–4 m house depth and no streets, the camera boom has less room.
